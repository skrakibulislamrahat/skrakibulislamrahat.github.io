Positioning

About

I do not frame medical AI as a leaderboard problem. I treat it as a reliability problem.

What I work on

Reliability before hype

My work centers on the gap between benchmark performance and dependable clinical use. That includes model miscalibration, shortcut learning from non-clinical artifacts, weak external generalization, and the need for evaluation protocols that reflect deployment conditions.

Current emphasis

Fundus imaging and diabetic retinopathy

Recent projects focus on diabetic retinopathy screening, retinal image artifacts, transfer of post-hoc calibration under dataset shift, and multimodal systems that combine images with structured clinical information.
