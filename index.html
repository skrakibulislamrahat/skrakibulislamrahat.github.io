Research thesis

The interesting failures begin where the benchmark ends.

My work follows one thread: a model is only useful when we understand what it learned, whether its confidence is warranted, and whether its prediction target still means the same thing when the data source changes.

APTOS 2019 · 5-fold stratified CV

Compact models, measured across folds—not one lucky split.

EfficientNet-B0, MobileNetV2, and SqueezeNet are compared using out-of-fold predictions and paired McNemar tests. The models are close enough that statistical comparison matters more than ranking them by a few thousandths of AUROC.
